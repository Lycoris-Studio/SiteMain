## Christmas Crashout
![/CCrashout.jpg](/CCrashout.jpg)

Christmas Crashout is a game made for UoL CSS' JollyJam 2025 and is a 2D Streets of Rage inspired game. You play as a Santa Claus imposter who has had a rough night at the pub, and wants everyone to know it. So you go out and cause mayhem in the streets, beating up anyone who gets in your way.

Play the [GameJam Build here!](https://lycoris-studio.itch.io/christmas-crashout)
